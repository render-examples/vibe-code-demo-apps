Above Muir Woods

Our retreat center sits in the hills above Muir Woods, Marin County — close enough to the redwoods to walk among them, far enough from the road to hear only birds.

Getting there

Exact directions and the driveway gate code are sent by email once your registration is confirmed. The site is a short drive from Highway 1, reached by a quiet, winding road through the hills.

Parking is limited to 40 spaces, matched to our 40-person capacity. Carpooling is welcome and appreciated.

Lodging

Stillwood is a day retreat — no overnight stay is included. Many guests choose to stay the night before in nearby Mill Valley or Stinson Beach and arrive rested. We're glad to share our favorite quiet places to stay upon request.
