Stillwood: A Silent Yoga Retreat

One day. No words. Just breath.

December 2, 2026 · 7:00am–4:30pm · Marin County, California

Reserve Your Place

A day of quiet

Spend a full day in noble silence, moving gently through yoga, walking meditation among the redwoods, and shared stillness with a small group of fellow seekers.

No talking. No phones. No agenda beyond the breath in front of you. Just forest, mat, and the slow unwinding of a mind that rarely gets to rest.

What the day holds

Arrival at 7:00am. Gentle yoga, silent meals, a guided walk through the redwoods, restorative practice, and a closing circle where words return at last. See the full schedule.

Your teacher

Elena Marsh

Elena is a longtime vinyasa and restorative yoga instructor who has led silent retreats across Northern California for over a decade. Her teaching is unhurried and precise — she trusts that stillness, given enough room, does most of the work itself.

Forty seats. One hillside. One day of quiet.
