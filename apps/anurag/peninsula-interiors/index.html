About Claire

Claire Whitfield founded Sandpiper & Sage Interiors after twelve years honing her craft, including several at a well-regarded San Francisco design firm. She started her own studio to work more closely with Peninsula families — the kind of clients who want their homes to feel as good as they look.

Her philosophy is simple: design should follow the way light moves through a room, the texture of the materials you touch every day, and the rhythm of how a family actually lives. Coastal-modern isn't a strict style so much as a mood — airy, warm, uncluttered, and built to last.

When she's not on a job site, you'll find Claire sourcing reclaimed wood at Peninsula estate sales or walking the Bay Trail with her dog, Sage.
