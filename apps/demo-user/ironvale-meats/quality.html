Quality & Safety

Inspected. Certified. Cold, End to End.

Our plant is USDA-inspected daily and HACCP-certified. Every cut is processed in temperature-controlled rooms and shipped in a continuous cold chain to guarantee freshness.

On the Floor

How We Keep Every Cut Safe

Our practices are built around one rule: control the temperature and control the handling, every step, every day.

  • Temperature-controlled processing rooms. Cutting and packing floors are held at a consistent, monitored temperature throughout every shift.

  • Daily USDA inspection. A federal inspector reviews the plant and product before, during, and after processing hours.

  • HACCP-documented critical control points. Every step where contamination risk is highest has a written control and a recorded check.

  • Unbroken cold-chain shipping. Product moves from our cold storage directly into refrigerated trucks — no exposure gaps before it reaches you.

  • Trained, certified staff. Every butcher and packer on our floor is trained in food-safety handling before touching a single order.
