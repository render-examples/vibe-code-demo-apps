Our Story

About Ironvale Meats

Four generations of hand-cutting, one uncompromising standard.

Since 1978

Built on a Butcher's Block in Cedar Falls

Ironvale Meats has been hand-processing quality beef, pork, and poultry for over 45 years. What started as a single family butcher shop is now a full-service USDA-inspected packing plant serving grocers, restaurants, and families across the region.

We were founded in 1978 in Cedar Falls, Iowa, and we've never moved. The same yard where our founder hung his first side of beef is the yard our trucks roll out of today — we've just added the cold storage, the inspection stations, and three more generations of family to the payroll.

"Our mission is simple: hand-cut every order like it's going to our own family's table, and never let a piece of meat leave this plant that we wouldn't serve at home." — The Ironvale Family
