Quality & Certifications

Safety and consistency aren't slogans here — they're inspected, audited, and documented every day.

Our Certifications

USDA Establishment #4471

Federal inspectors are on-site during all processing operations, verifying compliance with USDA food safety regulations on every shift.

HACCP Certified

Our Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points plan identifies and controls risks at every stage, from receiving to shipping.

SQF Level 3 Certified

Independently audited against the Safe Quality Food Institute's highest benchmark for food safety and quality management systems.

Halal Options Available

Select poultry and beef lines are processed to Halal standards. Ask your sales rep which SKUs qualify.

How We Keep It Clean

Every employee handling product completes food safety training before working the line. Equipment is sanitized between shifts under a documented Sanitation Standard Operating Procedure (SSOP), and cold-chain temperatures are logged continuously from processing through shipment.

Third-party auditors visit annually to verify our SQF Level 3 status, and USDA inspectors are present on-site during every processing shift — not just for periodic checks.
