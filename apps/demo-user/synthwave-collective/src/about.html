About the Collective

Synthwave Collective is a small, obsessive corner of the internet run by synth heads for synth heads. We're the kind of people who can identify a Juno-106 by its chorus alone, who have opinions about ladder filters vs. state-variable filters, and who will absolutely stop you at a flea market if we spot a vintage rack.

This site is our reference shelf: a catalog of gear worth knowing, patch recipes worth stealing, and events worth the plane ticket. No logins, no algorithms — just the good stuff, kept up to date by people who actually own the gear.

Got a synth story, a patch tip, or an event we should list? Find us at the meetups above — we're the ones near the modular table.

What We Believe

Analog Warmth Is Real

Whether it's oscillator drift or component tolerances, that "warmth" people talk about isn't just marketing — and we'll explain why.

Every Level Is Welcome

Don't know what a VCO is yet? You will by the time you leave this site. No gatekeeping, just good explanations.

Gear Is a Means, Not an End

The best synth is the one you finish a track on. We celebrate cheap clones and vintage classics equally.
