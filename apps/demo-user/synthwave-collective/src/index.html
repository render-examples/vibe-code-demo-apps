Why We're Here

We started Synthwave Collective as a place to swap patch notes, argue lovingly about ladder filters vs. state-variable filters, and keep track of which vintage machines are actually worth the asking price. No accounts, no noise — just reference-grade info from people who've owned the gear.

New to synths? Don't worry — we explain the jargon as we go. Veteran modular head? There's plenty here to argue with us about too.
