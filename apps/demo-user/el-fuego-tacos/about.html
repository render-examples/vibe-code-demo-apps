Our Story

It started with Abuela Reyes' salsa roja and a truck that barely ran.

Ten years later, the truck still barely runs, but the salsa is exactly the same.

El Fuego Tacos was founded in 2016 by the Reyes family in a converted 1978 taco truck — the kind that needs a prayer and a jump-start most mornings. What it lacks in horsepower, it makes up for in flavor.

Every tortilla is pressed fresh. Every meat is marinated overnight. Every taco is built to eat standing up, napkin in hand, sauce on your wrist. That's not a slogan — that's just how tacos are supposed to happen.

Meet the Pitmaster

Carlos Reyes has run the grill since the truck's first day in 2016 — trained by his mother, Abuela Reyes, on a backyard comal long before there was a truck to run. He still won't share the salsa recipe. Not with the cooks, not with his kids, not with anyone.
