Success Stories

Nothing warms our hearts (or wags our tails) more than a comeback story. Here are a few of our favorites:

🐶 Biscuit the Beagle

Before: Moving-day blues, hiding in moving boxes After: Zoomies through the new backyard in 3 weeks

Biscuit's family relocated across town, and he took it hard — refusing walks and burrowing into any box he could find. With Dr. Pemberton's gradual routine-rebuilding plan, Biscuit sniffed out his confidence again and now greets the new neighborhood like an old friend.

🐶 Nutmeg the Corgi

Before: Sulking after a new baby arrived After: Best big sister on the block

When a new baby suddenly became the center of attention, Nutmeg's short little legs carried her straight under the bed. Dr. Barkley's enrichment plan — plus some dedicated one-on-one playtime — helped her rediscover her joy, and now she and the baby are inseparable.

🐶 Gus the Great Dane

Before: Post-adoption sulking, ignoring his toys After: Undisputed neighborhood fetch champion

Gus arrived at his forever home a gentle giant with a heavy heart. Through play therapy sessions designed by Dr. Barkley, Gus rediscovered his love of fetch — and today, no tennis ball in the tri-county area is safe.
