Light-Up-Your-Day Resources

A few reads to brighten your pup's outlook — and yours.

5-Minute Sniff Walks

Turn an ordinary stroll into a nose-powered adventure. Letting your dog set the pace and follow their nose is one of the simplest mood boosters we prescribe — no appointment necessary.

The Power of a Predictable Routine

Dogs thrive on rhythm. Consistent mealtimes, walk times, and bedtime cuddles can work wonders for a dog whose world recently got shaken up.

DIY Enrichment: The Muffin Tin Game

Hide treats under tennis balls in a muffin tin and watch boredom melt into delighted snuffling. Dr. Barkley swears by it for the under-stimulated pup.

Grief in Dogs: What to Expect

Yes, dogs mourn too. Dr. Finch explains the stages of canine grief and gentle ways to support a pup who's lost a friend.
