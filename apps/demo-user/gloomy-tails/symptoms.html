Symptoms & Signs of Canine Low Spirits

Dogs are stoic little philosophers — they rarely announce a bad mood in words, so it's up to us to read the room (and the tail). Here's what to watch for:

  • Reduced interest in walks — even the mailman can't rouse them from the porch.
  • Hiding under furniture — the couch has become a fortress of solitude.
  • Ignoring favorite toys — Mr. Squeaky sits untouched, and that's just not right.
  • Excessive sleeping — more naps than a cat, and none of the enthusiasm.
  • Whimpering at odd hours — 3am blues are real, even for dogs.
  • Loss of appetite — when even bacon doesn't do the trick, it's time to take note.
  • Tail-tuck sulking — the universal doggy signal for "today is not my day."

If your pup is showing several of these signs for more than a couple of weeks, it might be time to bring in a specialist. Remember: tails will wag again, and every good boy (and girl) deserves to feel good.
