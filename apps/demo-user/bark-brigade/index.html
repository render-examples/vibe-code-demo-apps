How It Works

From first sniff to first bandana — here's the path every recruit walks (or trots).

  1. 1

    Fill Out the Enlistment Form

    Tell us about your good dog — name, breed, and their bravest moment so far.

  2. 2

    Attend Puppy Bootcamp Orientation

    A fun, low-key meetup where new recruits learn the ropes (and the hoses).

  3. 3

    Earn Your First Bandana

    Official Cadet status! Red bandana included, tail-wags mandatory.

  4. 4

    Join Real Community Events

    March, sniff, and greet at local fire-safety walks and neighborhood events.

Training & Ranks

Every recruit rises through the ranks with time, training, and treats.

🎽

Cadet

In training

Wears a red bandana and is still mastering the art of sitting still during roll call.

🥉

Junior Responder

1 year of service

Earns a small badge and can be trusted near the hose reel (mostly).

🥈

Full Firefighter

3+ years of service

Full badge and helmet. Sniffs out smoke like it's a squeaky toy.

🥇

Chief

5+ years of service

Gold badge, leads the pack, and gets the best spot by the fire truck.
