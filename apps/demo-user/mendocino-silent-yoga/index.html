Still Grove: A Silent Yoga Retreat

Saturday, December 2, 2026 · 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM · A redwood grove near Mendocino, CA

Reserve Your Place

One day. No words. Room to hear yourself.

Still Grove is a single-day retreat held among old-growth redwoods on the Mendocino coast. We gather at eight in the morning, set down our phones and our talking, and spend the day moving slowly through yoga, walking meditation, and silent meals. By evening, the silence lifts gently, in a closing circle, and we leave a little quieter than we arrived.

No prior meditation or yoga experience is asked of you. Only a willingness to be still.
