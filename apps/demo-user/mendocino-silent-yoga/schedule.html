Saturday, December 2, 2026

Ten hours, held loosely. Nothing is rushed. If a session runs long because the group needs the quiet, we let it.

  • 8:00 AMArrival & tea — settle in, find your mat space, warm your hands around a cup.
  • 8:30 AMOpening silence begins — a short instruction on note-passing for logistics only, then we stop speaking.
  • 9:00 AMGentle vinyasa — a slow, breath-led practice to loosen the body after travel.
  • 10:30 AMWalking meditation in the grove — a barefoot-optional walk beneath the redwoods.
  • 12:00 PMSilent lunch (provided, vegetarian) — eaten together, without talking.
  • 1:30 PMRestorative yoga — supported poses, blankets and bolsters provided.
  • 3:00 PMFree silent time / journaling — rest, write, or sit with the grove on your own.
  • 4:30 PMClosing circle — silence lifts, and we speak again, gently, if we choose to.
  • 5:30 PMTea & optional sharing.
  • 6:00 PMDepart.
